Timing is key.

Hint

2 / 6

Pellets will disappear on their own after a period of time, you may be on the right track but need to wait for a fresh pellet.

Hint

3 / 6

Using the black mark left by the bouncing pellet as a guide, fire a blue portal at the wall. Then wait for the pellet to enter the blue portal before firing another blue portal in a different location to redirect the pellet once it bounces back into the orange portal.

Hint

4 / 6

After the pellet emerges from the orange portal fire a blue portal at the orange light cast by the receptacle on the left wall then wait for the pellet to enter the receptacle.

Hint

5 / 6

Fire a blue portal on a wall where you can walk through it and step through onto the small platform, being careful not to fall in the water.

Solution

6 / 6

Fire a blue portal at the wall above the end of the moving platform's path and wait for it to line up below your portal before walking back through the orange portal and riding the platform to the chamber exit.
