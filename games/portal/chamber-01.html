Hint

The blue portal moves around the room every few seconds, changing where the orange portal sends you.

Solution

3 / 3

  1. Wait for the blue portal to appear in the room with the weighted cube before entering the orange portal.
  2. Take the cube back through the blue portal and wait again for the blue portal to move to the room with the button.
  3. Re-enter the orange portal and place the cube on the button before going back through the blue portal.
  4. Wait for the blue portal to move to the room with the door before entering the orange portal and proceeding through the door.
